WELCOME TO THE WORKBENCH

the moving parts.

01 / EXPLORE

Follow your curiosity.

Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, and right-drag to pan. Click any component or its name in the list. Focus, isolate, or switch to a cutaway to see inside.

02 / TAKE IT APART

One part at a time.

Assemble mode guides you through ten removable groups. Drag the highlighted part along its dotted guide, or use the step button. Switch to Put together to reverse the order. Undo is always there.

03 / SEE IT MOVE

A little motion explains a lot.

Run the assembled engine to see a slow-motion four-stroke cycle. The crankshaft, rod, piston, and valves move together. This is a simplified learning model, not a physics or service simulator.

04 / GO HANDS-ON

Leave the mouse for a moment.

Enable the camera and allow access. Move your index finger to aim. Quickly pinch and release to click. Hold a pinch and move to orbit or manipulate the next assembly part. Two pinched hands control zoom.
